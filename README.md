**Risk Engineer | AI & Automation | Hardware Tinkerer**

I'm a risk engineer working in a project management consultancy company, where overthinking is practically part of the job description. I'm interested in using technology to make everyday work a little smarter, and I spend my spare time building, breaking and experimenting with things — occasionally at the expense of my sanity and wallet.

### 🔧 Things I'm interested in

- **AI & LLMs** – AI assistants, agentic workflows, local models and AI governance.
- **Risk Intelligence & Digitalisation** – Risk analytics, quantitative risk modelling, Monte Carlo simulation, digital risk management and decision-support systems.
- **Hardware & IoT** – ESP32, electronics, sensors and little DIY experiments.

### 🧪 Things I've been building

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| Raebbt | A project risk-management application. |
| Risk Bot | An experimental agentic AI assistant for risk engineering, combining risk intelligence, workflow automation, persistent context and human-in-the-loop controls. |
| Borneo Fire Watch | A satellite-data-based fire monitoring and alerting experiment. |
| ESP32 Projects | IoT, DIY electronics and microcontroller programming. |

### 🧰 My current toolbox

- **AI & agents:** Claude, OpenAI, Hermes, local LLMs
- **Risk & analysis:** Excel, Monte Carlo simulation
- **Hardware:** ESP32, microcontrollers, basic electronics
- **Tools:** VS Code, GitHub

### 🌱 Currently learning

- Machine learning: predicting construction schedule delays with RiskML
- Agentic AI: designing agents that know which document to trust
- AI governance: how responsible AI applies to risk management
- Reading schematics and building an ESP32 tamagotchi from scratch

### 🧠 Outside of engineering

I read, I write poetry, and I chase new technology down rabbit holes. Some of my experiments stay small; some quietly grow into something bigger.

Almost all of them start with the same question: "But what if we tried this?"
